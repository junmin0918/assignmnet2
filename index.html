ABOUT MY PROJECT

CONCEPT

I wanted to create an interactive DJ-style media player where music and visuals work together. My aim was to make the experience feel more energetic and immersive than a traditional media player.

WHY PINK & PURPLE?

I chose pink and purple to create a neon, futuristic and nightclub-inspired atmosphere. These colours also create a consistent visual identity across the website.

WHY THIS MUSIC?

I chose these music because its strong rhythm and beat work well with the movement of the visual. The energetic sound supports the DJ and club-inspired concept of the project.

INTERACTIVE CONTROLS

The Play button starts the music and video. The Rewind and Forward buttons control the playback position. The Mute button controls the music, while Fullscreen allows the visual to fill the screen.

The vinyl disc can be dragged like a DJ turntable. Moving the disc creates a scratch sound and generates glitch effects. I developed the visual style myself because I wanted to create something unique and personal.

PLAYLIST

The Playlist button changes between different combinations of music and visuals. Each combination creates a different atmosphere while keeping the same overall visual style.

QUICK JUMP BUTTONS

The 1 and 2 buttons provide quick access to different moments in the visual. Button 1 jumps to 15 seconds, while button 2 jumps to 30 seconds of the video. This allows users to quickly explore different parts of the music and visual without manually searching through the timeline.
